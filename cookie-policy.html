What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your device through your web browser. They can help a website recognise a browser, maintain a session, remember preferences, or provide information about how the website is used.

Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, scripts, and other tools that store or access information on a device.

Types of cookies

Cookies can be grouped according to their purpose. The categories below describe the types that may be used on this website.

Category Purpose Consent
Strictly necessary Helps the website operate, maintain security, support basic navigation, or provide a function requested by you. May be used where necessary for the website to function.
Preferences Remembers choices such as language, display, or other settings. May require consent depending on how the technology is used.
Analytics Helps us understand visitor activity and improve the website. Will be used only where appropriate notice and consent are obtained.
Marketing Supports advertising, campaign measurement, or personalised marketing. Will not be used without the appropriate consent.

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers allow you to view, delete, block, or restrict cookies through their settings. You can usually find these controls under settings such as “Privacy”, “Security”, “Site settings”, or “Cookies”.

Blocking or deleting essential cookies may affect the operation of the website or prevent some features from working correctly.

Browser controls apply to the device and browser where the settings are changed. If you use multiple devices or browsers, you may need to configure each one separately.
